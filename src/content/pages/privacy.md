## Who we are

This website is operated by Exron Global General Trading LLC, [CONFIRM: registered address]. Questions about this policy can be sent to [CONFIRM: privacy contact email].

## What we collect

**Through the RFQ form:** organization name, contact person, email address, phone number, RFQ or solicitation number, product or service descriptions, line items and quantities, delivery destination, requested dates, additional instructions, and any documents you attach.

**Through the contact form:** your name, email address, subject and message.

**Automatically:** our hosting provider records standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested and time of request) to operate and secure the site. We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

## How we use it

We use the information you send to log and acknowledge your request, prepare a quotation, contact suppliers and logistics partners where needed to answer your requirement, and keep a record of our correspondence. We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

When we share part of a requirement with a supplier or freight forwarder to obtain a price, we share only what that party needs to quote.

## Services that process your information

- **Netlify** hosts the website and receives form submissions and attachments.
- **Google reCAPTCHA** checks that forms are submitted by a person. Google's privacy policy and terms apply to that check.
- **Resend** delivers the acknowledgment email and our internal notification.

## Sensitive and controlled information

Do not send classified information, Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) or export-controlled technical data through this website. The forms are not an approved channel for that material. If your requirement involves it, contact us first so we can agree an appropriate secure method of transmission.
